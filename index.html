Edge-Based FANUC CNC Condition Monitoring & Dispatch

  • Problem: Unplanned spindle/feed anomalies and manual maintenance logging on shop-floor CNC machines.
  • Approach: Built an Ethernet edge daemon interfacing directly with FANUC controllers via the FOCAS API (fwlib32.dll) to stream live telemetry into a rule-based alert engine and automated work-order logger.
  • Outcome: Real-time multi-tier fault visibility with automated maintenance dispatch logging.

CAD, Kinematics & Optimization

Adaptive Sheet-Metal Feeding System for Scrap Reduction

  • Problem: Fixed-pitch sheet feeding generates high strip-skeleton scrap and alignment variance in stamping lines.
  • Approach: Designed a 3D mechanical assembly and kinematic motion study paired with an analytical nesting and feed-pitch optimization model.
  • Outcome: Minimized material offcut waste while maintaining cycle-time synchronization.

Production & Assembly Line Digital Twin

  • Problem: Throughput losses and WIP buildup across stochastic multi-station assembly lines.
  • Approach: Formulated assembly precedence balancing (RPW/LCR) and built stochastic discrete-event & Monte Carlo simulation models to test buffer sizing and workstation allocation.
  • Outcome: Identified bottleneck stations and improved line efficiency under variable cycle times.

Structural/Thermal FEM & Statistical Process Control

  • Problem: Predicting thermo-mechanical deformation in forming/machining and controlling process drift.
  • Approach: Executed coupled thermal-structural and contact finite element analyses in ANSYS APDL/Mechanical alongside SPC capability studies (Cp, Cpk) and Taguchi DoE.
  • Outcome: Validated stress/temperature limits and established robust process parameter windows.
